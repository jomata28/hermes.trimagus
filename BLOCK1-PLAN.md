**Source-selection rules** (apply these when substituting any source above):
1. **Match lineage flavor to the target.** Warrior Monk Neigong is
   Buddhist/Shaolin. Ba Duan Jin and Yi Jin Jing both exist in Daoist-health
   and Shaolin-warrior versions — same name, similar shapes, different
   internal method. Prefer Shaolin-lineage teachers.
2. **Learn the shape free; buy the method.** Use free video for alignment,
   sequence, and mechanics only. Do NOT pre-load a competing breathing/
   internal method from a fifth teacher — it will have to be unlearned when
   the paid course is bought.
3. **Prefer teachers who stop and explain over follow-alongs.** A
   "12-minute morning qigong flow" builds nothing.
4. **One teacher per practice.** Do not sample three versions of the same set.

**Chinese-language bonus:** from ~Week 8, watch native-Chinese instruction
(search 二十四式太极拳教学 / 八段锦教学) — instruction becomes comprehensible
input, and from this point mine one new word every 10 minutes into Anki
(Ikenna Method upgrade trigger — see STATE-SHIFU.md LOG, 2026-07-24).

## MANDARIN METHOD — how to actually run each slot

The sources above say *what*; this section says *how*. Written so any
executor (or you on a tired morning) can run it without deciding anything.

### A. Day 1–2 only: the Ferriss deconstruction (one-time, ~2 hours total)

Translate these probe sentences into Mandarin (tutor, textbook, or a
reference grammar — not guesswork). The point is not to memorize them; it is
to see the **shape** of the language in one sitting before spending 6 months
inside it. Write each one down with what it exposed.

| Probe sentence | What it exposes |
|---|---|
| The apple is red. | 是 + 的 adjectival predication — and that adjectives don't need 是 alone |
| It is John's apple. | Possessive 的 |
| I give John the apple. | Basic SVO + double object |
| We give him the apple. | Pronouns don't decline — 我/我们, 他 stays 他 |
| He gives it to John. | Still no verb conjugation for person |
| I must give it to him. | Modal verbs (必须/得) stack before the verb |
| I want to give it to her. | 要/想 — desire vs. intention |
| I'm going to know tomorrow. | **No future tense** — time words (明天) do the work |
| I can't eat the apple. | Negation 不 vs 没, and 能/会/可以 distinctions |
| I have eaten the apple. | 了 (completed) vs 过 (experienced) — the single biggest beginner trap |
| I speak Chinese. | Topic-comment structure surfaces here |
| One apple / two apples. | **Measure words** (一个苹果) — obligatory, no English equivalent |

Expected conclusion, written into your notes: *no conjugation, no gender, no
plurals, no tense — but tones, measure words, and aspect particles carry the
load English handles with word endings.* That single sentence is your map.

### B. Anki (30 min, AM) — the procedure

1. **Due queue first, always.** New cards only after due = 0. If the due
   queue alone eats 30 min, that *is* the session — add zero new cards.
2. **New-card pace**: ~4/day wks 1–2 · ~7/day wks 3–4 · ~10/day wks 5+.
3. **Card format by phase**:
   - Wks 1–2: pinyin + audio → meaning. **No characters yet.** Sound first.
   - Wks 3+: character → pinyin + meaning (recognition), and separately
     meaning → character (production). Two cards per word.
   - Always include audio on the card. A word learned without sound is a
     word you can read and cannot use.
4. **Radicals before characters** (wk 3 onward). Learn the ~100 highest-
   frequency radicals as their own deck first. Then every new character is
   a *combination* of things you know, not a new drawing. 好 = 女 + 子, not
   six arbitrary strokes.
5. **Tag every card with its dynasty/unit** so vocabulary stays attached to
   the narrative it came from.
6. **Never edit the algorithm.** If retention drops below ~85%, reduce new
   cards; don't change intervals.

### C. Tone drilling (inside the Anki slot, wks 1–2; then 5 min/day maintenance)

Tones are the one thing that gets permanently harder to fix later. Front-load
them.

1. **Single tones** — 妈 mā / 麻 má / 马 mǎ / 骂 mà. Record yourself, compare
   against native audio, repeat until you can hear your own errors.
2. **Tone pairs** — this is the real drill. There are 20 two-syllable tone
   combinations (4×4 plus neutral). Practice them as a grid, not as isolated
   syllables — connected speech is where tones actually break down.
3. **Third-tone sandhi** — two 3rd tones in a row: the first becomes 2nd
   (你好 nǐ hǎo → ní hǎo). Drill this explicitly; it is never optional.
4. **Week 2 milestone gate**: self-record a tone-pair test, compare to
   reference. Do not advance to characters until you pass it.

### D. Input (60 min, PM) — the procedure

1. **Comprehensible, not aspirational.** Target ~90% understood. If you're
   looking up more than one word per line, the material is too hard — drop
   a level. This is the most common way people waste this hour.
2. **Two passes, not one.** First pass: listen/read straight through, no
   pausing, no lookups — build tolerance for ambiguity. Second pass: go back
   and mine.
3. **Word mining (from wk 8 movement / wk 10 history onward)**: one new word
   per 10 minutes → straight into Anki with the sentence it appeared in.
   Six words per session, not sixty. The sentence context is mandatory —
   isolated words don't stick and can't be reused.
4. **Shadow at least 10 of the 60 minutes** — play audio and speak along
   simultaneously, half a beat behind. This is the single highest-value
   pronunciation exercise available and it costs nothing.
5. **History-first ordering**: consume the era in English *first* if needed,
   then the same era in Mandarin. Knowing the story already is what makes
   the Mandarin version comprehensible.

### E. Speaking (30 min, PM) — the procedure

**On tutor days (4x/week):**
- Send the tutor a one-line agenda before each session. Pre-week-6: survival
  topics only (introductions, numbers, ordering, directions). Post-week-6:
  this week's history era and your movement practice.
- **Ask to be corrected on tones specifically**, not just grammar. Most
  tutors soften tone corrections unless asked. Say so in session one.
- Record every session (with permission). Re-listen to your *own* speech,
  not theirs.
- Last 5 min: have them ask you three questions you didn't prepare for.

**On non-tutor days (3x/week):**
- 10 min shadowing (same technique as input, different material)
- 20 min self-recorded narration: tell the week's history unit out loud in
  Mandarin, unscripted. Save the file. This is the retrieval practice the
  whole program is built on — comparing week 12's recording to week 3's is
  the clearest evidence of progress you will get.

### F. Decision rules (so no session stalls on a judgment call)

| Situation | Rule |
|---|---|
| Due queue > 30 min | Do due cards only. Zero new. Not a failure. |
| Retention < 85% for a week | Cut new cards by half until it recovers. |
| Can't understand this week's input source | Drop one difficulty level. Do not push through. |
| Tutor unavailable | Shadowing + self-recorded narration (Rule 9). |
| Tone error you can't hear yourself | Record, compare to native audio, bring to next tutor session. Don't self-diagnose. |
| Behind on the week's vocab target | Carry it forward; do not skip the era. Sequencing matters more than volume. |

## WEEK-BY-WEEK
**W1–2 · Sound system + skeleton**
- Mandarin: pinyin complete, 4 tones + tone pairs drilled daily, Ferriss 12 sentences, first 50 Anki cards (pinyin-only)
- Movement — **PHASE 1 · Mechanics** (15 min 站桩 standing, wuji posture per Damo Mitchell + 15 min mobility/deep squat). No forms yet. This phase is deliberately empty of choreography.
- History: Shang/Zhou overview (podcast, English)
- Milestone: pass a self-recorded tone-pair test; book iTalki tutor; hold standing 10 min without fidgeting; can distinguish 松 sōng (released) from collapse, and know where weight sits in the foot
- Reading: Lam Kam Chuen *The Way of Energy*; begin Damo Mitchell *A Comprehensive Guide to Daoist Nei Gong*

**W3–4 · Characters begin**
- Mandarin: switch Anki to characters (radicals first), HSK1 vocab ~150, first tutor sessions (introductions, numbers, survival)
- Movement — **PHASE 2 · First set** (12 min standing + 12 min 八段锦 Ba Duan Jin, standing version + 6 min mobility)
- History: Spring & Autumn, Confucius vs. Laozi — 道 dào enters your vocab from philosophy, not a textbook
- Milestone: 3-sentence self-intro from memory; all 8 brocades from memory without video; standing to 12 min
- Friday video-compare ritual begins here, applied to Ba Duan Jin

**W5–6 · Momentum**
- Mandarin: HSK1 complete (~300 words), graded reader #1 started
- Movement — **PHASE 3 · Form entry** (10 min standing + 8 min Ba Duan Jin, now the warm-up + 12 min Yang 24, movements 1–4: 起势 qǐshì, 野马分鬃 yěmǎ fēnzōng, 白鹤亮翅 báihè liàngchì, 搂膝拗步 lōuxī àobù)
- History: Warring States, Daoism deepens (无为 wúwéi, 气 qì — now your movement vocabulary IS your history vocabulary)
- Milestone: first 10-min tutor conversation with no English; standing 15 min; Ba Duan Jin clean enough to run on autopilot
- Reading: Tier 2 begins — Yang Jwing-Ming *The Root of Chinese Qigong*

**W7–8 · First integration**
- Mandarin: HSK2 vocab begins; describe movements to tutor in Mandarin
- Movement — **PHASE 3 cont.** (same split) — Yang 24 movements 5–8; native-Chinese instruction enters as comprehensible input (八段锦教学 / 二十四式太极拳教学), mine 1 word/10 min
- History: Qin unification, Legalism
- Milestone: standing 18 min; Yang 24 movements 1–8 continuous from memory

**W9–10 · Compression**
- Mandarin: HSK2 ~450 cumulative words; TeaTime Chinese podcast entry
- Movement — **PHASE 4 · Tendon work** (10 min standing + 8 min 易筋经 Yi Jin Jing, replacing Ba Duan Jin as the conditioning piece + 12 min Yang 24, movements 9–12)
- History: Han dynasty, Daoism institutionalizes
- Milestone: standing 20 min; Yi Jin Jing 12 movements learned; Yang 24 movements 1–12
- Reading: Yang Jwing-Ming *Qigong: The Secret of Youth* — read the Yi Jin Jing section while practicing it

**W11–12 · Consolidation + exam**
- Mandarin: HSK2 mock test; 15-min free conversation
- Movement — **PHASE 4 cont.** — consolidation only, no new material. Record final Block 1 video (standing posture + Yi Jin Jing + Yang 24 1–12) against the Week 3 baseline recording.
- IRL: attend one in-person class/trial in Houston (Month 2 checkpoint — mandatory correction session)
- Exit criteria (all four): HSK2 mock ≥80% · 40+ tutor sessions · 站桩 standing held 20+ min in one unbroken hold · both qigong sets (八段锦 + 易筋经) performed from memory, no video · Yang 24 movements 1–12 continuous from memory
- If met → Block 2 (HSK3 · Yang 24 movements 13–24 · standing to 30+ min · purchase and begin the Warrior Neigong course material if funds allow)
