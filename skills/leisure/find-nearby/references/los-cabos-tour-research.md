# Los Cabos Tour Research Notes

Use when the user is in Los Cabos/Cabo San Lucas and wants quick comparison shopping for tours, especially against hotel concierge offers.

## Location shorthand
- If the user says they are at a RIU in Cabo and asks about the "piedras" or rocks in front of the hotel, treat the likely target as Land's End / El Arco: El Arco, Lover's Beach/Playa del Amor, sea lion colony, Neptune's Finger, and Cabo San Lucas Bay landmarks.
- Do not assume the hotel offer is best. For groups, compare hotel pricing against direct operators and local marina/beach negotiation.

## Research workflow
1. Identify trip constraints first from the message: group size, hotel/location, last available day, family vs adults-only, and whether they want short/cheap vs full-day premium.
2. Pull direct operator pages first; search engines may bot-block VPS sessions. Useful operator sites found in-session:
   - Cabo Adventures: `https://www.cabo-adventures.com/en/tours`
   - Cabo Expeditions: `https://caboexpeditions.com.mx/`
   - Pez Gato: `https://www.pezgato.com/en/tours`
3. Extract total-cost factors, not just headline price: per-person price, dock fee, transport fee, park/ranch/entry fees, ages/weight restrictions, cancellation/weather policy, and whether pickup is included from the user's hotel.
4. For hotel-concierge comparisons, ask the user for a photo of the hotel tour sheet after giving a first-pass shortlist. Then compare line by line: hotel total vs direct booking vs negotiable local/private boat.
5. For groups of ~6, explicitly check whether a private boat/tour is possible or whether the listed tour is shared.

## Price/option anchors from June 2026 research
Treat as comparison anchors and verify live before booking:
- Cabo Expeditions Land's End Snorkel Adventure: about $52 USD pp, 1.5 h, ages 5+, year-round.
- Pez Gato Party Cruise: about $73 USD adult, 2 h, adults-only; in summer roughly 6:00–8:00pm.
- Pez Gato Jazz & Wine: about $109 adult / $54.50 child, sunset cruise; summer roughly 5:30–8:00pm.
- Pez Gato Mexican Flavors Sunset: about $99 adult / $49.50 child, dinner/open bar/sunset; summer roughly 5:30–8:00pm.
- Pez Gato Snorkeling Santa Maria Bay: about $99 adult / $49.50 child, 4 h; Mon/Wed/Fri around 10am, Tue/Thu/Sat/Sun around noon.
- Cabo Adventures Luxury Day Sailing: promo observed around $95.40 pp plus possible $4 dock fee and optional transport.
- Cabo Adventures Balandra Beach & La Paz Snorkeling: about $142 pp; full-day because of roughly 2.5 h each-way transfer plus boat/beach time.
- Cabo Adventures Combo Arch + Camels + Lunch: about $96 pp; good benchmark package when the hotel is separately selling an Arch boat and desert activity.
- Cabo Adventures ATV Desert Adventure: about $91 pp plus Tierra Sagrada entrance fee (observed $25 adult / $12.50 child).

## Presentation pattern
For travel/tour users on Telegram, give a practical ranked shortlist rather than a raw table:
- "Best cheap/nearby"
- "Best sunset"
- "Best snorkel"
- "Best full-day"
- "Best family/adventure"
Then include negotiation questions: final total with taxes/fees, transport, private vs shared, weather/port closure refund, and group discount for 6+.
