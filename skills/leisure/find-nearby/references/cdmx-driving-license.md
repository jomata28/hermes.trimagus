# CDMX driver license near Coapa/Tlalpan — session notes

Use this as a compact reference when the user asks where/how to get a driver's license in Mexico City, especially near Coapa/Tlalpan.

## Task shape

This is a hybrid of local-place lookup + government-procedure lookup. Do not only search for nearby offices; also identify the official procedure, payment source, and documents.

- Appointment entry point: `https://citas.cdmx.gob.mx/`

## Foreigner/resident framing

For a foreign resident in Mexico City, the practical checklist is:

- Passport vigente
- INM resident card, temporary or permanent, vigente
- CURP printed
- CDMX address proof, normally no older than 3 months
- Paid line of capture for the chosen license concept
- Originals + copies; screenshot/printout of appointment

Phrase it plainly: Mexican citizenship should not be required if they have legal residence, CURP, and CDMX address proof.
