# Constrained housing search near a landmark

Use this for apartment/house searches where commute, bedrooms, furnishing, and an all-in cap are hard requirements.

## Normalize the request first
Record separately:
- exact landmark/campus;
- maximum **total** monthly cost;
- exact number of real bedrooms;
- furnishing: required / preferred / unnecessary;
- maintenance included, known extra, or unknown;
- commute tolerance and whether late-night safety matters.

Do not infer that “no me gustaría que esté amueblado” means furnished or unfurnished without punctuation/context. In Spanish, restate the interpretation tightly: “Entiendo: ¿sí lo quiere amueblado?” when the distinction changes every result.

## Evidence levels
1. **Verified:** individual listing page is live and explicitly confirms rent, rental status, bedrooms, furnishing, and location.
2. **Conditional:** individual page is live but maintenance or furnishing is unknown. Keep outside the strict shortlist.
3. **Lead only:** search snippet/index card without an accessible individual page. Report separately, never mix with verified inventory.
4. **Rejected:** sale, duplicate, stale/404, wrong bedroom count, optional den counted as bedroom, or likely over all-in cap.

## Search sequence
1. Search the exact premium area.
2. Expand by commute corridors and safe pickup/drop-off—not concentric radius alone.
3. Search individual portals, local agencies, and medium-term rental sites.
4. For protected portals, use public result cards, indexed snippets, text-rendered pages, or a normal browser session. Never claim individual verification from a blocked index.
5. Open every promising individual page and quote the evidence for hard fields.
6. Deduplicate by URL, address/building, photos, price, area, and description.

## Portal lessons
- Large Mexican portals often protect index/detail pages differently. A blocked index does not imply individual pages are unavailable, and an accessible index does not prove details.
- EasyBroker/Pincali neighborhood result pages can expose card-level price, bedrooms, area, and individual URLs; inspect later pages, but open the detail page before marking furnished.
- Propiedades.com detail pages may be text-renderable even when browser navigation fails. Detail description is stronger evidence than index amenities.
- Homie exposes direct bedroom-filter URLs; a zero-result filtered page is useful negative evidence for current inventory.
- Airbnb monthly results require concrete dates and final monthly total including fees. A nominal price cap in the URL is not proof the UI honored it.
- Facebook Marketplace, Vrbo, Vivanuncios, Inmuebles24, Lamudi, and Mercado Libre may require login or anti-bot verification. Treat inaccessible results as unverified—not as no inventory.

## Budget discipline
- If rent equals the cap and maintenance is unknown, mark it **conditional**, not compliant.
- Compute per-person split only after establishing the all-in total.
- Do not flood the answer with attractive but noncompliant units. Give a strict shortlist first, then optional near-misses only if useful.

## Student/hospital screening
Ask the advertiser:
- still available today;
- exact maintenance and all-in monthly total;
- all bedrooms have door, window, and closet;
- exact furniture in each bedroom;
- three unrelated students accepted on one contract;
- guarantor/obligado solidario/póliza requirements;
- 24/7 security and safe internal rideshare pickup;
- exact pin for peak-hour and nighttime commute;
- video/live visit before any payment.

Never recommend a deposit before inspection, advertiser authority verification, and contract review.
