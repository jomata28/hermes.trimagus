4. Weekly review output ≤ 300 words, ends with exactly one corrective action for next week.
5. Never extend the plan silently; timeline changes require explicit user approval.
6. If the iTalki tutor isn't booked by Day 3 → escalate to Hermes; Output slot runs on shadowing + self-recording only until a tutor is booked (does not halt the rest of the program).
7. If a METRICS figure is flat across 2 consecutive Sunday reviews → swap the *encoding* for that track only (Anki card direction, or the Friday-comparison reference source for the stuck movement segment). Do not change dose or schedule to fix a plateau. Standing duration is exempt: plateaus there are normal and are addressed by holding the current duration longer, not by changing method.
8. Injury/inability → suspend the 30 min movement slot, redistribute those minutes to Input, until a session logs movement minutes > 0 again (self-evidenced resume, no approval step needed to pause or resume). Partial exception: if the injury permits standing but not the moving set (or vice versa), keep the permitted half rather than suspending the whole slot.
9. iTalki tutor becomes unavailable → fall back to shadowing + self-recording for up to 2 weeks; if still unresolved at week 3, escalate to Hermes and mark that week's speaking metric provisional — this does not block the Block 1 gate.
10. Do not pre-load a competing internal/breathing method from a source outside the four selected in BLOCK1-PLAN.md. Free video is for shape, alignment and mechanics only; the internal method is deferred to the paid course. Adding a fifth teacher's breathing method is a plateau-rule violation, not an enrichment.
11. Movement phases (P1–P4 in BLOCK1-PLAN.md) advance on the calendar, not on mastery — except standing, which is cumulative and never drops out. If a phase's set isn't learned on schedule, carry it into the next phase's warm-up slot rather than delaying the phase.
